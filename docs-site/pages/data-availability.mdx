Use this page to confirm that CBBD has the seasons needed for your project.
Choose a data family, check its range and notes, then open the linked API
operation to build a request.

## How to read the ranges

- Ranges are inclusive and describe records available on the verification
  date. New seasons and historical corrections can be added afterward.
- Basketball seasons use the **ending year**: `season=2026` means the 2025–26
  season, and `season=2027` means 2026–27. Recruiting classes, portal cycles,
  and NBA Draft years follow their own calendars.
- A listed season means records are available. It does not guarantee that
  every team, game, player, or response field is complete. Known gaps and
  sparse early coverage are called out below.
- An empty response or a missing field should not automatically be treated
  as a zero. Coverage also depends on the operation's filters and required
  underlying data.

## Games, schedules, and box scores

| Data                                   | Available seasons | API access                                                                                             | Notes                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| -------------------------------------- | ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Games, scores, and schedules           | 1949–present      | [Games](/api/games#getgames)                                                                           | Only 22 game records are available for 1949. Historical coverage varies.                                                                                                                   |
| Team season records and scoring totals | 1949–present      | [Team season stats](/api/stats#getteamseasonstats)                                                     | Aggregated from available completed games. Detailed box-score fields have a narrower range than wins, losses, and scores.                                                                  |
| Team box-score statistics              | 2003–present      | [Game team stats](/api/games#getgameteams), [team season stats](/api/stats#getteamseasonstats)         | Most 2003–04 box-score fields are missing. Populated shooting totals are limited to one game in 2003 and 12 games in 2004; coverage becomes much broader in 2005.                          |
| Player box-score and season statistics | 2003–present      | [Game player stats](/api/games#getgameplayers), [player season stats](/api/stats#getplayerseasonstats) | Player statistics with recorded minutes are available for only one game in 2003 and 12 games in 2004. Broader coverage begins in 2005; individual fields and players can still be missing. |
| Team statistics leaderboard            | 2015–present      | [Leaderboard](/api/stats#getteamleaderboardstats)                                                      | Stored season aggregates have a shorter history than the team season stats operation.                                                                                                      |
| Live scoreboard                        | Current games     | [Scoreboard](/api/games#getscoreboard)                                                                 | A live view with no historical season selector. Use the games operation for historical results and schedules.                                                                              |

Game lists and team box-score operations return at most 3,000 records per
request. Use team, conference, or date filters to retrieve smaller slices;
a single broad request may not contain every record in a season.

## Corrections

Historical source data can be corrected after publication. If a response
inside a listed range appears incomplete, include the operation, season, team,
and available game or player identifiers when reporting it through
[CBBD on GitHub](https://github.com/CFBD/cbb-api) or the
[CFBD Discord](https://discord.gg/Eb3ex5a).
